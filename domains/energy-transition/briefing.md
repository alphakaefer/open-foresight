## 5. Domain key factors: the bottleneck model

Usable output U = CES aggregate of the axes with rho = -3.0 (strong complements). U = **0.3137** with all axes, 0.4766 without permitting, linear mean would be 0.53.

| Grid (transmission and distribution) | 0.6 | 0.9 | 0.3158 | 0.7 % |
| Generation (PV and wind) | 1.0 | 1.5 | 0.3142 | 0.1 % |

**Binding bottleneck:** `permitting` – improving it raises usable output most.

> Evidence (permitting): In Germany, 400 GW of power and 661 GWh of capacity of battery storage are waiting for a grid connection, across 9,710 applications. Actually in operation: 2.3 GW and 3.2 GWh. Requested exceeds built by a factor of 170 (power) and 206 (capacity). In 2024 only about 25 GW received a commitment, roughly 6 percent of applications. The bottleneck is not the battery but the procedure before it.
